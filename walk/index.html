Plotline: The Landlord's Walk, Lizzie Magie's two rules on one street. Hold W or Up to walk; pay day comes twice a lap. Press E at a FOR SALE sign to buy. Rule 1: own 5 homes to win, and the people living there move out. Rule 2: a shared land tax, and nobody moves out. At a corner, Left turns to a cheaper town and Right to a richer one.
