## Before you submit

- [ ] Open the PowerPoint once and play slide 6 to check the video and sound on your computer.
- [ ] Check the audience line on slide 2 ("Made for anyone who has watched the forest at the edge of their town
      disappear under new buildings") says it the way you want.
- [ ] Practise once with a timer: the talk should be about 5 minutes.
- [ ] On the day: open https://hedytan.github.io/plotline_interative-media/build/ in Chrome, click to
      start, turn the volume up, press C for the camera and stand still for 2 seconds to calibrate.
      Keep the keyboard ready as a backup (Z / X chop, hold H / S / B build homes / shops / towers, T turn round).
